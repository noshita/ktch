# Glossary

Terminology related to ktch. 

## Theoretical morphology

```{glossary}
:sorted:

theoretical morphological model
  A model that mimics morphology and morphogenesis by mapping a few
  interpretable parameters to a form (Raup & Michelson 1965).

theoretical morphospace
  The parameter space of a theoretical morphological model, viewed through
  the forms the model generates. It covers forms that are theoretically
  possible but not observed, not only those that exist.

generating curve
  The closed curve swept along the {term}`growth trajectory` to generate the
  form, standing for the shape of the {term}`aperture` (Raup 1966).

growth trajectory
  The space curve traced by a reference point of the
  {term}`generating curve` as the organism grows; also called the generating
  spiral.
```

## Malacology

```{glossary}
:sorted:

aperture
  The opening of the last {term}`whorl` of the shell, through which the head
  and foot of the animal emerge.

  In ktch the term also names the cross-section the coiling models carry
  along the {term}`growth trajectory` — at any growth stage, not only at the
  final opening — including its orientation there (Noshita 2014).

coiling axis
  The axis about which the {term}`whorls <whorl>` of a coiled shell are
  wound.

  In ktch the term applies to Raup's model only: the growing tube model
  specifies its parameters locally, in a moving (Frenet) frame, and has no
  coiling axis.

whorl
  A complete 360° turn of the shell about the {term}`coiling axis`. The last
  and largest, which holds the body of the animal, is the body whorl. The
  earlier ones make up the spire.
```
